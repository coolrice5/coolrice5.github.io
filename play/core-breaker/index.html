How to dig

Click the rock (or hold SPACE) to hit it with your drill. Break a block and you drop one metre deeper.

Deeper layers are tougher but the minerals are worth far more — topsoil pays pennies, diamond pays fortunes.

Spend minerals on Upgrades for click damage and Machine Upgrades that dig for you, even while the tab is closed.

The Ascend bar at the top tracks the minerals you have earned this run. Reach a milestone and you may wipe everything for a permanent multiplier — entirely up to you.

Your drills kept digging

 

Ascend?

 

Everything else goes: depth, minerals, all upgrades, every machine past the Garden Shovel. Ascending is optional — you can keep digging this run as long as you like.

Reset everything?

This wipes your save completely — depth, minerals, every piece of gear, every machine, and every ascension multiplier you have earned.

There is no undo.
