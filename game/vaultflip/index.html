Vaultflip

One button. Flip gravity. Don't hit anything.

A one-button gravity-flip endless runner. Tap, click, or press Space to flip which way is down, dodge the neon blocks, grab coins and chase a high score. Instant load, works on desktop and mobile, nothing to install.

How to play

Does Vaultflip work at school?

It should. Vaultflip is 157 KB and is served straight from this site — it loads no third-party scripts, no ad SDK and no external assets, so there is nothing for a content filter to block piece by piece. It needs no download, no install and no account, and it runs on a school Chromebook. If this site's address is blocked on your network, nothing here can change that; the game itself is as small and self-contained as it can be.

Does my progress save?

Progress is kept in your browser on the device you played on. School devices often clear site data between sessions, so treat a long run as something to finish rather than resume.
