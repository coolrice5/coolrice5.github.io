Deep Fall

Descend twenty-one ocean zones to the abyss.

An idle descent clicker. Drive a submersible down through the water column, push through the pressure boundary at each metre, recover salvage and spend it on better equipment. Twenty-one ocean zones from the surface shallows to the abyssal core.

How to play

Click or tap to descend. Spend salvage on equipment.

Does Deep Fall work at school?

It should. Deep Fall is 294 KB and is served straight from this site — it loads no third-party scripts, no ad SDK and no external assets, so there is nothing for a content filter to block piece by piece. It needs no download, no install and no account, and it runs on a school Chromebook. If this site's address is blocked on your network, nothing here can change that; the game itself is as small and self-contained as it can be.

Does my progress save?

Progress is kept in your browser on the device you played on. School devices often clear site data between sessions, so treat a long run as something to finish rather than resume.
